# Demo photos, Puerto Cabello

This directory preserves two sets of third-party photographs: the original venue
photos requested for the live demo, and the Creative Commons alternatives added
later. Do not delete or silently substitute either set. Demo intent is not a
copyright licence; the original venue photos' redistribution rights have not
been established.
